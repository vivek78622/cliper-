What exactly am I buying?

A one-time ₹149 download with the complete copy-ready master prompt and a visual setup guide for automatic stream discovery, chat-scored clips, captions and automatic scheduling.

Is this a subscription?

No. The pack costs ₹149 once. The clipper is free and can be used without buying the guide.

Will it automatically post videos?

Yes. After the one-time setup, the AI runner discovers eligible live streams, scores highlights, creates clips and automatically schedules them to your connected accounts automatically. No per-video approval or manual posting is needed. Keep your computer on and online while it searches and clips; scheduled posts publish on time.

Can it send automatic DMs?

Optional workflows can automate eligible Instagram inbox replies or comment-to-DM flows after you connect and authorize the account. This is for supported inbound conversations and user-triggered flows, not unsolicited bulk DMs. YouTube does not have a direct-message feature.

Does the clipper use live chat?

Yes. Live chat reactions are the main highlight signal. When chat is unavailable or quiet, it falls back to audio and speech signals.

Is this an official Rockstar product?

No. GTA Auto Clipper is an independent fan-made project, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
